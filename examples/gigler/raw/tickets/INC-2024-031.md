# INC-2024-031: Transaction volume drop — 2024-08-12

**Severity:** P1  
**Status:** Resolved  
**Owner:** platform-data@gigler.io  
**Opened:** 2024-08-12  **Resolved:** 2024-08-14

## Summary

Weekly transaction volume dropped 34% week-over-week (W32 → W33 2024).
dqt STL anomaly detector fired at 09:14 UTC Monday. On-call engineer paged.

## Root Cause

A depleted inventory of mid-range gig listings in the "Writing & Translation" category
caused avg_price_usd to spike +48% in W32. Buyer demand collapsed the following week
(consistent with the gig-price → transaction-volume causal edge, lag=1w, r=-0.55).

Secondary factor: marketing_campaigns.spend_usd for the "Writing" segment was cut by
$12,000 in W31 — this contributed approximately 18% of the volume drop (estimated via
Shapley attribution over the confirmed causal DAG).

## Timeline

- 2024-08-05: marketing spend cut for "Writing" segment (-$12k)
- 2024-08-06 – 2024-08-09: avg_price_usd rises +48% ("Writing & Translation")
- 2024-08-12 09:14 UTC: STL residual z-score = 4.2 → incident opened
- 2024-08-12 10:30 UTC: causal trace identifies gig_prices.avg_price_usd as primary driver
- 2024-08-14: listing inventory restocked; spend restored; incident resolved
